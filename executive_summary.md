# Executive Summary: Product Line Profitability & Portfolio Margin Performance
**Target Stakeholders:** Executive Committee, Board of Directors & Operations Leadership  
**Distributor:** Nassau Candy Distributor  
**Dataset Scope:** 10,194 Wholesale Orders (2024–2025) | 15 Catalog SKUs | 5 Production Facilities  

---

## 1. Strategic Mandate & Headline Financials
A comprehensive profitability audit was executed across Nassau Candy Distributor's 10,194 transaction records to resolve structural margin dilution and SKU over-extension. The portfolio delivered:

- **Gross Revenue:** \$141,783.63 across 38,654 shipped units.
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** \$48,340.83.
- **Gross Profit Realized:** \$93,442.80.
- **Portfolio Gross Margin:** **65.91%**.

While aggregate gross margin appears robust, granular unit-level accounting reveals extreme profit concentration, hidden subsidies, and margin destruction in non-core divisions.

1. **Extreme Pareto Vulnerability (Top 5 SKUs = 95.06% of Profit):**  
   Five Wonka Bar chocolate SKUs (*Scrumdiddlyumptious*, *Triple Dazzle Caramel*, *Milk Chocolate*, *Nutty Crunch Surprise*, *Fudge Mallows*) generate **\$88,824.62 (95.06%)** of total corporate profit on **92.88%** of revenue, at a 67.45% average margin. The remaining 10 SKUs generate just **\$4,618.18 (4.94%)** combined profit.
2. **Value-Destroying Contract: Kazookles (`OTH-KAZ-38000`):**  
   *Kazookles* generated \$1,205.75 in revenue across 371 units, but consumed **\$1,113.00 in direct manufacturing cost** (a **92.31% cost-to-sales ratio**), yielding a dismal **7.69% gross margin** (\$92.75 total profit; \$0.25/unit). Sourced from *The Other Factory*, it costs \$3.00/unit to manufacture against a wholesale realization of \$3.25. When factoring warehouse pick/pack labor, *Kazookles* operates at a severe net operating loss.
3. **Sub-Scale Administrative Overhead in Sugar Division:**  
   The Sugar division records an attractive nominal gross margin (68.10%), but generated only **\$348.73 in revenue and \$237.48 in profit across 34 orders** over two years. Processing orders for items like *Fun Dip* (3 orders, \$4.80 total profit) costs far more in order processing and freight handling (\$15–\$35 per PO) than the gross margin captured.
4. **Manufacturing Hub Centralization:**  
   95.06% of total profit is concentrated in two contract factories: *Lot's O' Nuts* (Arizona, 56.47% profit share) and *Wicked Choccy's* (Georgia, 38.58% profit share). Any localized outage at either facility represents a catastrophic operational risk.

## 3. Executive Decision Roadmap (ROI-Prioritized)

### Days 1–30: Triage & Cash Conservation
- **Terminate or Renegotiate Kazookles:** Issue a contractual ultimatum to *The Other Factory* to reduce unit cost from \$3.00 to $\le$\$1.25. If rejected, de-list the SKU immediately.  
  *Impact:* Instantly frees \$1,113.00 in cash flow and eliminates 96 unprofitable warehouse order cycles.
- **Implement Sugar Division Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ):** Mandate full-case orders (minimum \$250 aggregate PO) on *Fun Dip*, *Nerds*, and *SweeTARTS* to prevent sub-scale order processing.

### Days 31–90: Commercial & Pricing Restructuring
- **Price Escalations on Sub-60% Margin SKUs:**  
  - Increase *Fun Dip* wholesale list price from \$1.50 to \$2.05 (+36.7%), lifting gross margin from 40.00% to 56.10%.  
  - Increase *SweeTARTS* and *Nerds* pricing by +25.0%, moving margin from 46.67% to 57.33%.  
- **Contract Renegotiation on Lickable Wallpaper:** Sourced from *Secret Factory* (\$7,860 sales, 50.00% margin). Renegotiate unit COGS from \$10.00 down to \$7.50 to capture an incremental **\$982.50 in net profit**.

### Days 91–180: Operational Realignment
- **Facility Sourcing Rationalization:** Transition or consolidate production out of *The Other Factory* (11.87% net facility margin) and *Sugar Shack* (\$220.98 total revenue) into *Secret Factory* or co-packing partners.
- **Capital Reinvestment:** Reallocate liberated working capital into capacity guarantees and product line extensions for core Wonka Bar Chocolate franchises.
